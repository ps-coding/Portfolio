AI-Assisted Discovery of Physics Laws: Practical Improvements and Considerations

2025

First author of eleven · NJ Governor's School in the Sciences at Drew University · Best Poster, IEEE MIT URTC (first out of approximately 700 submissions)

In this paper, we reimplemented the core of AI Feynman from scratch and tested it on data with added noise and varying numerical ranges. We also introduced an adaptive-α rule for symmetry detection, which detected nearly twice as many true symmetries (110 compared to 60) and reduced false positives tenfold (3 compared to 29).

Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) Noise Resistance

2025

Sole author · preprint on Cambridge Open Engage, not peer-reviewed · 534 views, 242 downloads, and 5 citations on Google Scholar as of November 2026 · DOI

In this study, I tested two multimodal LLMs (GPT-4o and LLaVA) and a text-only baseline (GPT-3.5) on prompts with textual noise and image noise. Noise reduced the performance of the multimodal models more than that of the text-only model, and, contrary to my hypothesis, denoising the prompts with a spell-checker or an image denoiser reduced performance further.
